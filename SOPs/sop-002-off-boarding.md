# SOP-002 - Employee Offboarding

## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard process for removing an employee's access to Acme Solutions Ltd. systems when employment ends.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all employees leaving the organization.

## Prerequisites

- HR has submitted an approved offboarding request.
- Employee's final date and time have been confirmed.
- Manager has identified any required data or resource transfers.

## Procedure

1. Receive the offboarding request from HR.
2. Verify the employee's identity and termination date.
3. Disable the employee's Active Directory account.
4. Remove or restrict the employee's access to company resources.
5. Revoke active sessions and access where applicable.
6. Preserve required company data according to organizational requirements.
7. Transfer required files or ownership to the appropriate manager.
8. Recover company-owned equipment.
9. Verify that the account can no longer authenticate.
10. Document completion of the offboarding process.

## Verification

- Employee account is disabled.
- Access to required company resources is removed.
- Company equipment has been recovered.
- Required business data has been preserved.
- Offboarding activity has been documented.

## Documentation

Record the completed offboarding activity in the appropriate IT ticket.
