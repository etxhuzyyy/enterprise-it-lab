# Acme Solutions Ltd. - Company Profile

## Company Overview

Company Name: Acme Solutions Ltd.
Industry: Software Development & IT Consulting
Location: Ottawa, Ontario, Canada
Employees: 150
IT Staff: 1 Sole IT Administrator

Acme Solutions Ltd. is a mid-sized software development and IT consulting company that provides custom software solutions and technology services to business clients.

## Business Environment

The company operates from a single Ottawa office. The sole IT administrator is responsible for managing the organization's network, servers, user accounts, endpoints, security, backups, technical support, and IT documentation.

The environment is designed to demonstrate how one IT administrator can design, deploy, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain an enterprise IT environment.

All systems and scenarios in this project are simulated for educational and portfolio purposes.
